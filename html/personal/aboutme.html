    I made this site to showcase what programming I can do and what things I can create. Specifically, I wanted a place where people on the internet can see all of my work and get to know me more (cuz I'm not much of a talker).

    I really like the y2k asthetic, but I wanted a site that was more focused on keeping the website accessible and platform agnostic. This website is built on the philosophies of y2k and simplicity rather than crazy new platforms that are difficult to fix later on.
