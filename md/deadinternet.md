# Reddit
After spending a long time on Reddit, it is interesting that content is commonly
recycled. After all, this is why DailyDoseOfInternet has been so popular. A
collection of content curated by real people gets the attention of everyone.

based on text and images, bot APIs are a thing, and engagement is good for the
ad revenue, the content has been slowly been becoming worse on the most popular
subreddits.
