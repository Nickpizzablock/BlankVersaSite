# ♥ Breakcore ♥
Breakcore is an electronic music genre focused on 
hard-hitting, rhythmic basslines, chill vocals, and 
generally an intense, but focused vibe.

I initially got into breakcore from Sewerslvt's Mr. 
Kill Myself. At first, I thought the song was way too 
chaotic and loud. But, ironically, the music allows 
me to be more focused and alert. It can also be 
relaxing as you lose yourself in the techno beats and 
drum lines. It is definitely an experience and a 
acquired taste.

I find the feeling similar to the lofi genere but 
using a totally different technique. While lofi aims personal/aboutme.html
to use calming instrumentals typically quiet and 
casual meant for focus, breakcore aims to drown your 
head with crazy noises that distances you away from 
the outside world. Somehow, both generes help me 
focus whether it be driving, studying, or having background noise.

by Imogen Heap. I learned of Imogen's existence 
because of this track. The album art is sick and 
the pitch makes the vocals sound angelic.

Another memoriable moment was the track "turmoils of time".
The first time I listened to this, I was on the trolley going back home. 
The sunset was just over the horizon, so everything looked amber. Ocean waves were glistening in the background while 
buildings cast shadows onto the windows of the trolley. There was a gentleman and a lady dressed in dirty rags taking up the 
bench seating. Their tattered backpacks sat beside them. The woman's head was on the guy's shoulder looking very relaxed. 
The man had his head bowed down seemingly resting. The whole scene looked peaceful.
Despite all the hardships, they found a moment of piece on this train ride. This 
exact song was playing on my earbuds while I idlely gaze at them, taking in this moment. 

One more song I'd like to acknoledge is "something complete". Another Imogen Heap 
bootleg with breakcore fills while bringing the nightcore asthetic in a different 
direction. The chaos of the track brings calmness to me where I can just focus on 
the music while I let everything around me happen. Just letting the time pass 
feels so relaxing when the brain can hyper focus on one sense and just experience it. 

## Zhoni

My absolute favorite track is "Eternal Rest Palace" under the album Ultranostalgia https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzNINUbhjFc. Very intense and on edge. 
The 4/4 beats make it rhythmic and the synths have thy gamery type vibe. 
The song gives a sense of falling. This is the song I use if I ablsolutely need 
to lock in. 

"Unconciousness" is another track I like. It has heavy, pounding bass in a 4/4 time. 
It gives a sense of speed like the only thing that matters is what is in front of 
you. The intense rhythms make it feel a bit like it was meant for a rhythm game. 
It also has some ghoulish vibes with the samples in the song, 


"i finally broke the core" in webcore fiends feels very rhythm gamey with tons
of recognizable samples. With the y2k theme, the track feels like it belongs in 
DanceDanceRevolution.
